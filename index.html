The idea

One intelligent commerce workflow

E-commerce tools are fragmented: research lives in one place, suppliers in another, creative and advertising somewhere else — and what gets learned rarely travels back. We're building AI E-commerce Operator to connect these stages into one coherent workflow.

Today Fragmented

Context is lost at every hand-off between tools, tabs and spreadsheets.

  • Trend tools

Product Discovery

Many signals. One clear opportunity.

We're designing the discovery stage to look across many weak signals and converge on products worth a closer look — with the evidence attached.

  • Market signalsDesigned to watch demand and interest indicators from public sources.
  • Trend discoverySpot rising themes early, and separate lasting shifts from short spikes.
  • Product researchTurn a raw idea into a structured, comparable product profile.
  • Evidence gatheringKeep sources and reasoning attached to every claim.
  • Opportunity evaluationAssess candidates against explicit, reviewable criteria.

Store Operations

Advertising

Powerful, but never unsupervised

We're designing advertising workflows around control: campaigns are prepared by the system, then reviewed and approved by a person before any budget is spent.

  • Campaign preparationStructure, audiences, creative sets and settings drafted for review.
  • Controlled budget executionSpending designed to stay inside limits you set.
  • Approval workflowsSensitive actions are designed to require explicit validation.
  • Campaign monitoringOngoing visibility into delivery and results.
  • Advertising APIsDesigned to connect to platform interfaces where appropriate access is available.

The system is designed to be able to integrate with advertising platforms — for example TikTok API for Business or Meta's marketing APIs — through their official interfaces, once appropriate access has been granted. The system

Built around specialized AI agents

Rather than one monolithic model, the platform is designed as a team of focused agents, each responsible for one part of the workflow and handing structured context to the next.

A1

Research Agent

Designed to scan market signals and collect evidence on emerging opportunities.

  • Signals
  • Trends
  • Evidence
A2

Product Intelligence Agent

Structures raw research into comparable product profiles and opportunity assessments.

  • Profiles
  • Scoring
  • Risks
A3

Sourcing Agent

Designed to find and compare supply options, with details prepared for human review.

  • Suppliers
  • Comparison
  • Lead times
A4

Creative Intelligence Agent

Develops concepts, marketing angles and hooks adapted to each channel.

  • Concepts
  • Hooks
  • Formats
A5

Media Agent

Designed to generate and adapt image and video assets from approved creative direction.

  • Images
  • Video
  • Variants
A6

Acquisition Agent

Prepares campaigns and budget plans. Execution is designed to require approval.

  • Campaigns
  • Budgets
  • Approvals
A7

Measurement Agent

Collects and normalizes performance data into one consistent, trustworthy view.

  • Metrics
  • Reports
  • Anomalies
A8

Learning Agent

Turns results into recorded learnings that shape the next decision.

  • Diagnosis
  • Memory
  • Decisions

Agents describe the intended architecture. They are under development and not all are available.

About

An independent project, still taking shape

AI E-commerce Operator is an independent software project in development. It explores how AI and automation can orchestrate complex e-commerce workflows while keeping humans in control of the decisions that matter.

Everything on this site describes the product vision and design direction. It is not a description of a released product, and it should not be read as a claim about customers, revenue, partnerships or certifications — there are none to report.
