Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

In short: this website has no user accounts, no forms, no analytics, no advertising trackers and no marketing cookies. We do not collect personal data through the site itself.

1. Who we are

AI E-commerce Operator is an independent software project in development. This website presents the project and its vision. It is operated by the individual who owns the project; there is no registered company or team behind it at this time.

You can contact us by email at the contact address that will be published here soon.

2. What this website does — and does not — do

  • It is a static website: pages are delivered as-is. There is no backend, no database and no login system.
  • It has no user accounts and no forms that send data to us.
  • It uses no cookies and does not use browser storage (such as localStorage) to track you.
  • It uses no analytics, no advertising pixels, no social-media trackers and no marketing technologies.
  • It loads no third-party scripts, fonts or media. All code and assets are served from the same website.

3. Hosting and technical logs

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. When you visit any website, your browser necessarily sends technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. GitHub may process this data to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not receive or have access to a log of individual visitors from this hosting service. You can read how GitHub handles data in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If the site is later served from a custom domain, the domain name registrar and DNS provider may also process technical data needed to resolve the domain.

4. If you email us

If you choose to contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message, along with whatever information you decide to include. We use it only to read and answer your message. We do not sell it, share it for advertising or add you to any mailing list without your request. We keep correspondence only as long as it is useful to handle your request, then delete it.

5. Links to other websites

This website may link to third-party websites or services (for example GitHub). Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control. Please review their policies before sharing any information with them.

6. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data through the site, there is generally nothing to access or delete. If you have emailed us and would like a copy of your correspondence, or would like it deleted, contact us and we will act on your request. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

7. Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

8. Changes to this policy

If the website starts to handle personal data — for example by adding analytics or a contact form — we will update this page before doing so and change the “Last updated” date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at the contact address that will be published here soon.
